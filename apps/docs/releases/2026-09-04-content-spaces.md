Every project and dataset now belongs to a space — your personal space or a team's — instead of floating free under one account. Sharing a project keeps you as its owner and only grants access, while transferring a project moves it into someone else's space for good, which is the tool to reach for when a project changes hands permanently. The Content page's Recent view shows what you've opened lately across every space you can reach, and Shared with me collects everything other people have shared with you in one place.
